Journal of psychopharmacology (Oxford, England) · Adverse drug reactions associated with psychotropic medications combined with versus without lithium: A case/control pharmacovigilance analysis.

#5 💊 高實用性

經證據支持的鋰鹽或拉莫三嗪在停用丙戊酸藥物後90天內開始使用，可提升育齡女性雙相情緒障礙患者的藥物治療成功率，降低復藥風險
